> **Warning for readers and agents:** this canvas is for the team's earlier idea, a shift-aware commute check, **not for RideRecord**. The team pivoted on November 13, 2026 and has not written a canvas for RideRecord.

# Business Model Canvas

Shift-aware commute check for Detroit workers without a car

## 8. Key Partners

- Michigan Works! and the state labor department
- Regional transit authority, DDOT and SMART
- Shift employers: plants, warehouses, hospitals
- MichiVan vanpools and SMART Flex
- IBM and Google (technology)

## 7. Key Activities

- Keep bus schedule data current and re-score commutes
- Tune the commute risk score
- Run the agent that reads a job posting for location and shift
- Outreach to employers and agencies

## 2. Value Propositions

- Yes, risky or no verdict on the round trip to a specific job and shift, before accepting the offer
- Risk score that shows why: transfers, wait between buses, margin before the last bus home
- Backup plan when the answer is no: vanpool, on-demand shuttle or a different shift
- Employers see which shift times cut off applicants without cars

## 4. Customer Relationships

- Free and self-serve for job seekers, no account
- Saved commutes re-checked when schedules change
- Dashboard and onboarding for employers and agencies
- Each verdict shows the schedule data behind it

## 1. Customer Segments

- Detroit job seekers without a car weighing an offer for a specific shift (21% of Detroit households have no car)
- Suburban employers hiring shift workers
- Michigan Works! counselors
- Later: transit agencies planning around shift times

## 6. Key Resources

- Open DDOT and SMART schedule data
- Google Maps transit routing
- IBM watsonx: Granite and Orchestrate
- Employer shift-time data

## 3. Channels

- Michigan Works! offices and counselors
- “Check your commute” link on job postings
- Community organizations and libraries
- Mobile web app, no install

## 9. Cost Structure

- Cloud hosting and AI model usage
- Maps routing calls beyond the free tier
- Development and data upkeep
- Employer outreach and sales

## 5. Revenue Streams

- Free for job seekers
- Employer subscription, per site or per posting
- Licenses for workforce agencies
- State mobility grants to fund the pilot

*Revenue streams and partners are assumptions to validate with employers and workforce agencies.*
